---

## Why Thermal Imaging Matters
Thermal imaging reveals problems that are invisible to the naked eye:

- Overheating electrical components  
- Faulty solar connections  
- Water intrusion  
- Insulation gaps  
- Structural anomalies  

Early detection prevents downtime, reduces repair costs, and improves safety.
