## What Is IEC 62446-3?
IEC 62446‑3 is the international standard for thermographic inspection of photovoltaic (solar) systems.

It defines:
- Inspection procedures  
- Image capture requirements  
- Environmental conditions  
- Reporting standards  

## Why It Matters
Compliant inspections ensure:
- Accurate diagnostics  
- Reliable documentation  
- Repeatable results  
- Industry‑accepted reporting
